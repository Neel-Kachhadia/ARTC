Sol High read-only review of the proposed Phase 4 validation plan

Disposition: use a bounded, risk-based execution set; do not claim that every
item in the broader fault catalog passed. Every catalog item needs PASS, FAIL,
NOT APPLICABLE with a reason, or NOT RUN with an exact reason.

Material plan adjustments:

1. One AttemptManager cannot have an active hedge and pending retry at once:
   retries are scheduled only after active attempts reach zero. Exercise the
   shutdown compound as concurrent logical-request cohorts; do not describe it
   as one request state.
2. Retain packet loss + backend restart (mandatory compound C-011) as one short
   bounded recovery experiment or mark it NOT RUN. Distinguish TCP loss from
   injected application UNAVAILABLE because loss may cause deadlines without a
   retryable gRPC status.
3. Include overload + retryable errors + reduced deadlines (C-012) in the
   low-budget retry incident or explicitly mark it NOT RUN. Keep counters that
   separate injected application failures from transport effects.
4. Caller-cancellation storms are not available in artc_loadgen. Exercise
   cancellation/hedge races with the existing deterministic held-back-backend
   harness; do not equate a cancellation-ignoring backend with caller churn.
5. The load generator stores at most 10,000 decision samples. At 500 RPS and
   one in 100 decisions this caps near 33 minutes. The soak must use sparse or
   segmented decisions and check final outstanding callbacks, leases, timers,
   attempts, and route inflight.
6. `lab/run_phase2_experiments.sh` suppresses qdisc and stress cleanup errors;
   that is not fail-closed. Fix/verify cleanup or do not rely on that script for
   Phase 4 evidence. Compose teardown alone does not prove measured recovery.
7. Require explicit healthy -> fault -> recovery times, exact targets and
   injection acknowledgments, seed/event log, cleanup proof, invalidation on
   generator lag, and a calibrated offered-load base before calling a case 5x.
8. A one-hour healthy soak is credible if the Phase 2 30-minute evidence is
   reused, resource monitoring has adequate samples, and post-fault/steady-state
   behavior plus final drain are checked. Extend only on suspicious evidence.
   Five-to-ten independent matched trials are appropriate for selected headline
   comparisons; do not repeat a full baseline ladder for every matrix cell.
9. A2 root egress netem affects A2->B as well as router->A2. Do not claim an
   isolated router-to-replica link fault from that injection. Service B delay
   is configured at startup; restarting B to change it adds a process fault.
10. Mark external exporter outage NOT APPLICABLE because the repository has no
    exporter/collector; dynamic removal/reload is NOT APPLICABLE because the
    configured replica set is static. Missing fuzz targets, allocator-failure
    injection, unsupported packet modes, and 6h+ soaks are NOT RUN, not passes.
    CI automation absent from this repository is NOT RUN, not a local gate.

Suggested compact retained set:

- burst + single slow A2, including matched hedge-off/on trials;
- burst + all replicas slow;
- packet loss + finite retry budget, plus deterministic UNAVAILABLE budget test;
- Service B slowdown + A2 CPU saturation, with dependency calls verified;
- short deadlines + load increase and a small deadline-gate ablation;
- recovery + immediate load spike, with eligibility, limit, and settling traces;
- deterministic caller-cancel/hedge, deadline/retry-timer, and shutdown-fence
  interleavings;
- selected multi-faults A (burst + A2 CPU + B delay), B (loss + UNAVAILABLE +
  low budget + reduced deadlines/elevated load), C (straggler + hedge + caller
  cancellation in test harness), D (recovery + spike), and E (shutdown across
  separate active-hedge/retry/cancel request cohorts).

Also require checks for recovery/reintegration, post-cancel work separately for
cancellation-aware/ignoring servers, route/attempt/timer/lease bounds, qdisc and
process cleanup, and a real process shutdown smoke. Keep negative results such
as deadline-gate no-benefit, AIMD settling limits, and unsupported p999 visible.

No product feature or algorithm redesign is necessary to execute this set.
