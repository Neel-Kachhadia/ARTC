ARTC open-loop generator through pinned TSan router
===================================================
TSAN_OPTIONS=halt_on_error=1:history_size=7
Three TSan-instrumented Service-A processes used a 50ms Alarm delay behind a
TSan-instrumented round-robin router. The load generator scheduled 200 calls
at 100rps with max-inflight 64 and a 100ms issue-lag limit. The 10ms arrival
interval overlaps the 50ms service delay; the observed latency p50 was 79,167us.

Result: scheduled/issued/completed=200/200/200; errors=0; valid=true;
generator_saturated=false; max issue lag=541us; response counts A1/A2/A3=
67/67/66; all four server processes exited 0 after SIGTERM; TSan reports=0.

A 300rps TSan pilot using a 20ms service delay completed 600/600 calls with no
TSan report, but max issue lag reached 75,154us against its 50,000us limit.
Its manifest correctly marked it generator_saturated=true and valid=false; it
is retained as a saturated pilot, not counted as a pass.

Complete process logs and both manifests are retained under
/tmp/artc-pinned-tsan-generator-concurrency/.
